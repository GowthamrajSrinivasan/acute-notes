Meaning labelling

Would a reader of the card alone come away believing something the source does not say?

All three files are in src/ai/fidelity/__fixtures__/meaning/. Nothing leaves this browser.

Rubric, short version
LabelTest
FaithfulA reader who acts on the card makes the same decision as one who read the source. Shorter is fine; stronger, wider, reversed or new is not.
PartlyMostly right, but one arguable detail is lost or bent. You'd want the author to look, but can't point to a clearly false claim. Always say why.
DistortedYou can name the false belief in one sentence: a hedge removed, a condition or scope dropped or widened, a negation flipped, cause and effect swapped, a number or unit changed, a cost dropped or inverted, an absolute added.
UnsureYou can't decide. Say what you'd need to know. It is discussed like a partly.

Judge meaning, not style. The card stands alone: don't fill in from the source. One false belief is enough. Hedges are equivalent within their class (can / may / might / could; usually / typically / generally). Numbers are exact after formatting (1,000 = 1000 = one thousand). A requirement for N means at least N unless an exact count is stated; a limit ("up to 3") is different. Full rubric: .ai/docs/meaning-labelling-rubric.md.
